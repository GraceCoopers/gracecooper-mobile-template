From assignments to adulting.

Trying to balance uni, money, work and having an actual life? Yeah, it’s a lot. Here’s your little guide to finding your feet and making the most of this chapter without completely losing your mind.

04. Finding your balance

Don't leave everything until the night before. I know I'm definitely not the only one who does this! Keep track of your hand in's, break assignments into certain hours of the day, and start early. Future you will thank you.

We all know as soon as that student loan comes in it's pretty hard to resist the urge to spend it all at once. Set aside money for rent, food, and bills first, then give yourself a little budget for fun! You can still enjoy uni without being broke by Wednesday.

Learn a few simple meals to cook, keep your space clean so you can still function properly. Remember to be respectful of your living space as you would want others to be respectful of yours. And always remember to take care of yourself!

It's easy to get caught up in both grinding out assignments and the excitment of the uni life. That's where it's all about balance. Make time for your friends, try new things and give yourself a break when you need one.

The adulting checklist:

○ Know when rent is due

○ Have at least three easy meals you can cook

○ Keep track of uni hand in's

○ Make time for yourself

○ Start the uni assignment before the night it's due

○ Remember to buy toilet paper before you run out
