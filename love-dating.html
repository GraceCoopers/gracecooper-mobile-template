Love & Everything in Between.

So.. you’ve entered the dating world. Good Luck.

Whether you're in a relationship, single, or something abit complicated, there's no right timeline for finding your person. xo

Your twenties are about learning what you like, what you don't, and what you deserve. You don't need to have your love life figured out, and you definitely don't need to compare yours to everyone else's.

Quick Tips:

❤︎⁠ If they’re giving mixed signals, take the signal and move on.

❤︎⁠ Keep you’re standards high, but don’t expect someone to be perfect.

❤︎ Don’t ignore the red flags.

❤︎ If they only want you when it’s convenient for them, let them be inconveniently single.

Tip #1 Always make time for the girls.
